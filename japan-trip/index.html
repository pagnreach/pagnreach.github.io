💡 Essential Japan Pro-Hacks

📲 Apple Wallet Digital Suica

Open Apple Wallet on iPhone ➔ tap + ➔ Transit Card ➔ Suica. Top up with your credit card. You can tap your phone through every train turnstile in Osaka, Kyoto, and Tokyo, and pay at 7-Eleven and vending machines.

🚄 Mount Fuji Shinkansen Seat Hack

When riding the bullet train from Kyoto to Tokyo on Dec 4, book Seats D & E on the 2-seat side. Around Shizuoka Prefecture (~45 mins before Tokyo), you will have a panoramic view of Mount Fuji out your window.

🧳 Yamato Luggage Forwarding (Takkyubin)

Don't haul heavy suitcases onto the bullet train. On Dec 3 at your Kyoto hotel front desk, ask them to forward your suitcases to your Ginza hotel (~$18/bag). Travel on the Shinkansen with just a light backpack!

🚕 The $6–$8 Kyoto Short Taxi Rule

Kyoto buses are notoriously packed with tourists. For short 5-minute hops between your hotel and Gion or Kiyomizu-dera, hail a taxi on the street or use the GO app. At $6–$8 USD for two people, it's worth every penny.
